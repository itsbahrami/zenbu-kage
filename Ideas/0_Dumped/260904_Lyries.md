# Lyries

Lyricist Slides is a slide‑based lyric workspace where each slide holds up to 5 configurable text slots—you enable only the slots you need and label each one from a preset list (e.g., "Japanese", "Romaji", "English", "Spanish", "فارسی"), optionally tagging them with semantic flags like "primary", "translation", or "original" so you know their role at a glance, but the tool stays dead‑simple because fonts and text direction (RTL/LTR) are automatically applied based on the label you choose (so picking "Japanese" gives you Klee One and LTR, picking "فارسی" gives you Vazirmatn and RTL) with no manual font‑picking or direction toggles required; you paste or type content into each slot per slide, preview the slides in a clean full‑screen view, adjust global text size, and print or export to PDF, with all projects saved locally in your browser—no media player, no timestamps, and no complex column matrix, just an effortlessly multilingual lyric‑writing and presentation tool.
